---
title: Email that sells
---
Every email has a subject line, a preview text and a body. One story, one idea and one call to action per email.

## Sequences
- Welcome, after an opt-in: deliver the freebie, tell your story, share a quick win, then invite them to the next step.
- Launch: follow the launch phases, with every email written in full and dated.
- Buyers: onboarding on day 0, 1, 3 and 7, so they start and get a first result.
- Non-buyers after a launch: empathy, a downsell and a free resource.
- Abandoned checkout: a reminder within the hour, then an answer to the top objection the next day.

## Subject lines
Short, specific and curious. Write five, pick one, and keep the promise of the subject line in the first two lines of the email.

## Writing the body
Write like you talk to one person. Short paragraphs, one link, a P.S. that repeats the call to action. Match the length and tone of your best past emails.
