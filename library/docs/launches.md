---
title: Launch phases
---
A launch is a set window where an offer opens, gets attention and closes. Plan it backwards from the close date.

## Pick the launch type
- Under 500 people in your audience: a seed launch. Sell by hand to a small group and prove the offer.
- 500 to 5,000: an internal or webinar launch to your own audience.
- 5,000 and up, with a proven product: a partner launch with affiliates.
- A community goal: a challenge over three to five days.
- Hands off: an evergreen funnel with a nurture sequence.

## The four phases
1. Pre-prelaunch: hints, behind the scenes posts and a waitlist.
2. Prelaunch: three free pieces of content. The opportunity, the transformation and the experience of what it is like inside.
3. Open cart: doors open, the value stack, proof, objections, a bonus that expires, the final 24 hours and three emails on the last day.
4. Post-launch: onboard the buyers, survey the people who did not buy and offer them a downsell or a free resource.

## The launch math
Sales needed equals the goal divided by the price. Visitors needed equals the sales needed divided by the conversion rate. Example with made up numbers: a $10,000 goal at a $500 price needs 20 sales. At a 2 percent conversion rate, that is 1,000 visitors to the sales page.

## Launch emails
Write every email in full with its send day, its subject line and one call to action. Use real scarcity only: a real close date, a bonus that really ends, or a real cap on spots.

## Webinar launches
Hook, your story, three teaching points that each break one false belief, then the offer and live questions. After the webinar, send three replay emails over 48 hours.
