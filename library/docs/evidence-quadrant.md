---
title: The Evidence Quadrant
---
The Evidence Quadrant sorts proof into four types. A page that covers all four feels safe to buy from. Use real proof only.

## The four types
- Internal direct: your own results, experience and before and after stories.
- Internal indirect: data, research and a story that shows why you see the problem clearly.
- External direct: testimonials, reviews and case studies from customers.
- External indirect: signals from the outside world, like engagement, shares, press, partners and growth.

## Where proof goes
Put proof right after the claim it supports. A results section after the promise, a testimonial next to the price, a case study before the guarantee. When you are new and have no customer results yet, use concept work, your own projects, a free pilot with a few people, or data from a trusted source, and say plainly what it is.

## Urgency without pressure
Give a real reason to act now: a launch close date, a bonus that ends, limited spots for something you deliver by hand, or a price that goes up on a set date. Say why the limit exists.

## Risk reversal
A risk reversal moves the risk from the buyer to you. Write it as a short promise: what you guarantee, for how long and what they do to claim it. Put it under every buy button and in the FAQ.
