---
title: "DM sales: Connect, Collect, Close"
---
The 3x3x3 system closes sales in direct messages: three stages with three moves each. Every message earns the next reply.

## Connect
1. Open with something specific to them, like a post they wrote or a result they shared.
2. Build rapport with a real question about their world.
3. Show quiet authority with one short line about the work you do, with no pitch.

## Collect
1. Ask where they are now.
2. Ask what is in the way.
3. Ask where they want to be. One question per message.

## Close
1. Bridge with their own words: you said this is the problem and this is where you want to be.
2. Present the transformation and what is included before the price.
3. Handle the top objection and ask softly, like: want me to send the details?

## The 3 Cs of closing
Connect, Collect and Close also work on calls. Audit their situation first, then prescribe the solution like a doctor. Sell the vision of the result, then agree on scope, price and start date. If it is not a fit, say so. That honesty is a big reason people say yes when it is a fit.

## Proof, price and availability
When people hire, they want three things fast: examples of the work, the price and when you can start. Give them those three early and keep messages short.

## Platforms
- Instagram: short and casual, with voice notes once the conversation is warm.
- LinkedIn: no pitch in the first message.
- WhatsApp: personal, one idea per message.
- Facebook: keyword triggers on posts can start conversations for you.

## Objections
Validate the concern, clarify what is really behind it, reframe it and ask again. Too expensive often means the value is still unclear, so go back to what the result is worth to them.

## Follow-up
Follow up on day 1, 3, 7 and 14, each time with an easy way out. Never use guilt.
