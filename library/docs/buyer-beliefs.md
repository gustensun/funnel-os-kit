---
title: The 12 buyer beliefs
---
Before someone buys, they need to believe twelve things. A sales page, a VSL or a sales call should build each one.

1. They want the outcome and value it.
2. They admit they have the problem and want it solved soon.
3. They believe your solution can solve it.
4. They trust that you are the right person to help.
5. It makes sense to them why they have not solved it yet.
6. They see why your process is better than what they tried.
7. They see that people like them got results, which gives them hope.
8. They see that the value is worth more than the price.
9. They are willing to make time for it.
10. They see that doing nothing is the bigger risk.
11. They know exactly what happens next.
12. They feel a reason to solve it now.

## Using the list
Go through a page or a call script and mark where each belief gets built. A belief with no section behind it is usually where people drop off. Proof sections build beliefs 4 and 7, the price anchor builds 8, the guarantee builds 10 and the next steps section builds 11.
