---
title: "Copy: awareness stages and structures"
---
Good copy starts with the reader. Name the goal of the piece, who reads it and how aware they are, then pick the structure.

## The five awareness stages
- Unaware: they do not know they have the problem. Lead with a story or a pattern interrupt.
- Problem aware: they feel the pain. Name it in their words.
- Solution aware: they know solutions exist. Show why this method is different.
- Product aware: they know your offer. Give proof and answer objections.
- Most aware: they are ready. Make a direct offer and remove friction.

## Pick a structure
- PAS, problem, agitate, solution: ads and emails.
- AIDA, attention, interest, desire, action: cold pages.
- Before, after, bridge: transformations.
- PASTOR, problem, amplify, story, transformation, offer, response: long sales copy.
- Story: warm audiences.
- Direct offer: retargeting and people who already know the product.

Pages follow the 17P outline for their type.

## Hooks
The first line calls out the problem, the promise or the opportunity. Write three hooks with different angles, then pick the one you would stop for.

## The Winning Ad Formula
A structure for long form ads: a hook that stops the scroll, why they should listen to you, a high perceived value and then the lower price, the easy button, proof, a tease of what they do not know yet, how simple it is, a metaphor they can picture, the steps, the name of the system, the effort that went into it, the bonuses, exactly what to do, real urgency, what happens after they buy, and the risk reversal.

## A VSL script in five parts
1. A polarizing hook that challenges what they believe.
2. The old method they use now and why it keeps failing.
3. The new method, your different way to the result.
4. The proven system: the steps and the proof that it works.
5. The unique opportunity: the offer and why now.

Keep it conversational, as if you talk to one person. Read it out loud and cut everything that sounds like a commercial.

## Stories that sell
Open in the moment, show the struggle, show the turning point, show the result, then connect it to the reader's situation. A story lets people feel the before and after for themselves.

## Objections to answer
Too expensive, no time, they do not trust you yet, it looks complicated, it will not work for them, maybe later, and who are you. Answer the ones that apply right where the doubt shows up on the page.

## Copy that sounds human
Conversational and first person, short paragraphs, specific numbers, no hype, no exclamation points and no filler. Use exact offer names, prices, guarantees and dates, and never invent results. When you write with AI, give it samples of your own writing, your offer details and the structure, then edit the draft line by line.
