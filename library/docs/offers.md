---
title: The 3T Offer Triangle
---
The 3T Offer Triangle tests an offer on three sides before you build a funnel for it. A funnel only sells an offer that is already clear.

## The three sides
- Transformation: who goes from what painful before to what after. Say it in one sentence: I help [who] go from [before] to [after].
- Timeline: how fast they get there, with milestones along the way. A clear timeline makes the result feel reachable.
- Trust: the proof that it works and the guarantee that makes it safe to try.

Score each side out of 10. Fix anything under 7 before you write the page.

## The Golden Offer Quadrant
Score the offer against the alternatives with four questions. New: is it a new way to get the result? Better: does it get a better result? Faster: does it get there in less time? Convenient: does it take less effort and fewer steps, or is it done for them? An offer that wins on one has a reason to exist. One that wins on several is easy to sell. If it wins on none, fix the offer before you write the funnel.

## The value stack
Write the offer as numbered text. The core product comes first, then each stack item with a plain name, a one line outcome and its value. Each bonus removes one specific reason not to buy: a done for you template for no time, a group call for low confidence, a setup video for no tech skills.

## The price anchor
Show the value before the price, so the number lands against the value. A five layer anchor, using only true numbers: what people normally pay for this result, the total value of the stack, the value of the items sold separately, the regular price and today's price.

## Guarantees
A guarantee moves the risk from the buyer to you. Pick one you can keep: money back within a set number of days, a results guarantee with clear conditions, or a promise to keep helping until they get the result when they do the work. Write it in plain words under every buy button.

## Pricing
Price from the outcome. Ask what the result is worth to the buyer over the next twelve months, then price at a fraction of that so the decision is easy. Then check the price against the funnel: the right price is one your funnel can sell profitably.

## Productized services
A productized service is a service with a fixed scope, a fixed price and a fixed timeline, like a funnel build in 14 days or a monthly content package. It is easier to sell because the buyer knows exactly what they get, and easier to deliver because every project follows the same steps.
