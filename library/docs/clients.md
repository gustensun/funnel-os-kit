---
title: Getting your first clients
---
Clients come from proof and conversations. Build the proof first, then start the conversations.

## Position, Promotion, Partner
- Position: pick one growing market with buying power, one clear offer and a small portfolio.
- Promotion: create magnets like a free audit, a template or a small tool, send gift first outreach, and post content that compounds.
- Partner: close by auditing their business, acting like a consultant and prescribing what they need. Then retain them and grow into bigger work.

## Start with people who know you
Make a list of thirty people who already know you, send each a personal message and offer to build something for them first. The first clients usually come from your own network.

## A portfolio with no clients
Build concept projects. Pick real brands or creators in your market, design what their funnel or page could look like and show it. Add your own live projects. A portfolio page needs a one line promise, the concept work, client work when you have it, your process and one clear call to action. A strong concept piece often lands the first client.

## Gift first outreach
Send something made for them before you ask for anything: a redesigned hero section, a short audit video, three headline rewrites. Show what you did. Keep the message short, skip the long intro and ask for an easy yes.

## Picking a niche
Pick a market where people already spend money and where you can reach the buyers. Wealth, health and relationships are the classic three. Choose an industry you care about, because you will be in it for years.

## Communities and referrals
Go where your clients already gather: online communities, groups and the comment sections of people in your niche. Help first. For referrals, ask right after a client gets a result, and make it easy with a short message they can forward.
