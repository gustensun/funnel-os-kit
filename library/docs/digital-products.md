title: Digital products
---
A digital product packages a transformation so it can be sold and delivered without your time for every buyer.

## Input, method, output
The simplest structure is their input, your method and their output. A meal planner, a workout builder, a pricing calculator or a content planner all follow it. AI helps at every step: research the market, draft the method, build the tool, write the sales page and create the content.

## Your first product
Pick one transformation in a market where people already spend money. Make something they use every week, like a tool, a template, a planner or a calculator. Validate before you build too much: talk to buyers, presell or run a small test. Keep the first product simple, specific and quick to deliver.

## Pick the format by the need
- They need to learn a skill once: a short course.
- They need to do a task again and again: a template or a tool.
- They need support over time: a membership or a community.
- They need accountability and feedback: coaching or a cohort.

## Pricing a digital product
Price from the result and from where it sits on the ascension ladder. Low ticket at $7 to $47 works as a front end that pays for ads. A product that saves real time or money every week can carry a monthly price.

## Selling without a big audience
A low-ticket funnel with small paid tests, partners who already have the audience, and helpful posts in communities where your buyers gather. Each sale also shows you which message works.

## Micro tools
A micro tool is a small app that solves one problem: a calculator, a generator, a planner or an audit. It works as a lead magnet, a product or a bonus, and people value it because they get a result in seconds. With an AI builder you can make one in an afternoon.

## Why most courses fail
They teach everything they know when the buyer wanted one result. Cut every lesson that does not move the buyer toward that result, add a quick win in the first day, and add tools and templates so they can apply it.
