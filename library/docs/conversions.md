---
title: The CONVERSIONS checklist
---
CONVERSIONS is an eleven point check for any page that sells. Read the page once for each letter and mark what is missing.

1. Clear outcome: the headline says the result in plain words, for a specific person.
2. Opportunity selling: the page sells the future the buyer gets, with the features as support.
3. New method: the page explains why this way of getting the result is different from what they tried.
4. Value proposition: one sentence says what they get, how fast and why it is worth it.
5. Empathy: the page shows you understand their situation in their own words.
6. Results: real outcomes with numbers, names and timeframes, where you have them.
7. Social proof: testimonials, reviews, logos, case studies and community size.
8. Irresistible offer: a stack, bonuses and a price anchor that make yes the easy choice.
9. Objection handling: the top doubts get answered where they show up, plus an FAQ.
10. Next steps: one clear action, repeated, with what happens after the click.
11. Scarcity: a real deadline or limit. Only use scarcity that is true.

## How to use it
Score each letter as present, weak or missing. Fix the missing ones first, starting from the top of the page, because most visitors never reach the bottom. Then pair it with the 17P outline for the page type, which tells you where each fix belongs.
