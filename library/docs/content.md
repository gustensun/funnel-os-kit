title: Content, personal brand and YouTube
---
Content does two jobs: it brings the right people in, and it builds the trust that makes them buy later. Ground every idea in a real signal and point it at something you offer.

## The CONTENT mix
Seven types of content that grow a business:
- Customer results: proof through success stories and projects.
- Offer differentiation: what makes your way different.
- New discoveries: lessons you are learning right now.
- Thought leadership: truths you believe in, even unpopular ones.
- Education and entertainment: helpful tips that sound like a person.
- Narrative framing: stories, metaphors and analogies.
- Trends and news: be the source for what is happening in your industry.

Mix all seven and your feed builds proof, authority and connection at the same time.

## An idea worth making
Every idea gets a working title, a hook for the first line or the first five seconds, the angle, why it should work (a post or video in your niche that did far better than usual), and which offer or freebie it points to. Study outliers and repeat the pattern with your own angle.

## YouTube
- Titles: under 60 characters, specific, with a curiosity gap the video pays off. Write ten options and pick the one you would click.
- Thumbnails: one focal point and three to five words that add to the title.
- Scripts: a hook in the first 5 to 10 seconds that confirms the title, a short setup, chapters with an early quick win, and one call to action.
- Numbers: a click-through rate under 4 percent usually means a packaging problem with the title and thumbnail. An average view duration over 50 percent is good.
- Consistency: a steady schedule you can keep beats bursts, because every upload teaches you something.

## The 12 principles of personal branding
Personality, Authority, Positioning, Credibility, Pricing, Relevance, Engagement, Framework, Portfolio, Association, Endorsement and Celebrity. The first group is how people see you: likable, expert, essential and credible. The middle group is how you show up: pricing like a specialist, speaking their language and building real engagement. The last group is leverage: a named framework, a portfolio of proof, who you are associated with, who endorses you, and in time being known in your space.

## Posts and announcements
Lead with the most interesting line, keep one idea per post and end with one clear next step. To announce a new offer, say what it is, who it is for, the outcome, the proof and how to get it, and show it in action with a screenshot or a short video.
