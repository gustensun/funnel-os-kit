---
title: Ascension and the Profit Maximizer
---
The Profit Maximizer is an ascension model. Every customer gets a clear next step, so each one becomes worth more over time and you can afford to reach more people.

## The four levels
- Free: lead magnets and freebie funnels that bring people in and build trust.
- Low ticket, $7 to $47: a mass market offer that turns strangers into customers and pays for the ads.
- Mid ticket, $100 to $1,000: automated offers with more help and more access.
- Back end, $1,000 and up: high-ticket coaching, done for you work or a program, sold through an application or a call.

The front end gets the customer. The back end grows the business. Each level should solve the next problem the customer has once the last level worked, which makes the next step feel natural.

## The low-ticket funnel
Map the steps before you write copy: the front end offer, an order bump on the order form, upsell 1 (a quick result accelerator), upsell 2 (often recurring, like a membership or software), the confirmation page, and then the back end email sequence that invites buyers to the high-ticket offer.

## Bundle stacking
One core product that solves one problem, three to seven stack items that make the result faster or easier, and two or three bonuses that remove friction. Give every item a name, a one line description and a value.

## The break-even math
Blended order value equals the front end price, plus the bump take rate times the bump price, plus each upsell take rate times its price. When the cost to get a buyer is under the blended order value, the funnel pays for itself on day one.

Example with made up numbers: a $27 front end, a $17 bump taken by 30 percent of buyers and a $97 upsell taken by 10 percent gives 27 + 5.10 + 9.70, so $41.80 per buyer.

## Headline tests
For split tests, write up to 20 headline variants by rotating four parts: the opening hook, the credential, the deliverable and the benefit.

## The 7 step flywheel
Find, Convert, Upsell, Cross Sell, Ascend, Retain and Ignite. Find the right people. Convert them with a first offer. Upsell more of what they just bought. Cross sell something related that gets them the result faster. Ascend them to the higher offer. Retain them by keeping them getting results. Ignite referrals and content from happy customers. Most businesses only work on Find and Convert. The other five steps are where each customer becomes worth more.
