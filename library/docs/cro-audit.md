---
title: Conversion audit order
---
Fix a funnel in order of impact. Most of the lift comes from the first three steps, and no amount of button testing fixes a weak offer.

1. The offer.
2. Message to market match: the ad or post and the page make the same promise to the same person.
3. Page structure: the 17P outline for that page type.
4. Trust and proof.
5. Friction: long forms, unclear steps, surprise costs.
6. Technical: load speed, the mobile layout, broken links.
7. Small details: button colors, icons and microcopy.

## Find the weakest step first
Read the real numbers for each step: opt-in rate, sales page conversion, order form completion, upsell take rate and refunds. Rough ranges to compare against: opt-in pages 20 to 45 percent, sales pages on cold traffic 1 to 4 percent, order forms 50 to 75 percent, upsells 10 to 25 percent. These are rough guides. Your own past numbers are the better benchmark.

## Audit the page
Go through the CONVERSIONS checklist, the 17P outline for the page type and the Evidence Quadrant. Name the top three issues and fix those first.

## Write the test
Write every test as one sentence before you build it: because [observation], we believe [change] will [outcome] for [audience]. We will know when [metric] moves by [amount].

Rank tests by impact, confidence and ease. Test one change at a time, run each test for full weeks and wait for 95 percent confidence before you call a winner. Keep a log of every test and what it taught you, so the next page starts from your winners.
